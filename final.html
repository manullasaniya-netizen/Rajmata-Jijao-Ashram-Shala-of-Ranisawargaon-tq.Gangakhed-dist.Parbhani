Our Facilities

Hostail Facilities

The hostel provides students with comfortable accommodation and a supportive living environment. It helps students develop independence, discipline and good study habits while staying away from home.

Mess

Our mess provides students with fresh, hygienic and nutritious meals in a clean and comfortable dining environment. It aims to maintain proper food quality and cleanliness while ensuring that students receive healthy and balanced meals every day.

Play Ground

Our playground provides students with space for outdoor games, sports and physical activities. It encourages teamwork, cooperation, sportsmanship and active participation in extracurricular activities.

Library

Our library provides students with a peaceful learning environment filled with educational books, reference materials and study resources. It encourages reading habits, independent learning and knowledge development. Students can use the library to improve their understanding, explore new subjects and prepare for their examinations.

Science Lab

Our Science Laboratory provides students with opportunities to explore scientific concepts through practical experiments and activities. It helps students develop observation, analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. The laboratory supports learning in subjects such as Physics, Chemistry and Biology and encourages curiosity and scientific understanding.
